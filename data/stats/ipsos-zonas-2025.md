# Ipsos zones of Lima 2025 (`ipsos-zonas-2025.csv`)

Source: Ipsos Perú, "Las 6 caras de Lima Metropolitana 2025" (Perfiles zonales 2025), September 2025. Ipsos cites its own studies and INEI ENAHO 2023.
File: https://www.ipsos.com/sites/default/files/ct/publication/documents/2025-09/Perfiles%20Zonales%202025_V3.pdf

Indicator: `zone_household_income_pen`, the average monthly income of a household in the zone, in soles.

| Zone | Income | Page |
|---|---|---|
| Lima Norte | S/ 4,069 | 3 |
| Lima Este | S/ 3,951 | 4 |
| Lima Centro | S/ 4,307 | 5 |
| Lima Oeste | S/ 8,258 | 6 |
| Lima Sur | S/ 4,309 | 7 |

The district list of each zone is in `places.csv`, copied from the footnote of each page. The five zones cover the 43 districts of Lima province once each. The sixth Ipsos zone, Callao, is left out, because the platform covers Lima province only.

Ipsos is a private market research firm. These values are context only.

All 11 app districts sit in Lima Oeste, so this figure does not separate them. It shows how that zone compares with the rest of Lima.
